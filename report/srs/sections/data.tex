\section{Main data}

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}L{0.26\textwidth}Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Entity} & \textbf{Key fields} \\
\midrule
Donor & id, name, type, email, phone, active \\
Donation & id, donor, type, date, reference number, status (plus amount, check number for checks, or item details for in-kind) \\
Payment & id, payer, amount, fee, currency, method, status \\
Volunteer & id, name, email, phone, available \\
Skill & id, name \\
Event & id, title, event type, date, capacity, hours, status \\
EventRegistration & id, event, attendee name and email, ticket reference, checked in \\
VolunteerEventAssignment & id, volunteer, event, skill, work date, hours \\
Beneficiary & id, name, phone, household size, need type, status \\
Distribution & id, beneficiary, donation (optional), item, quantity, date \\
EventType & id, name (editable lookup for events) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

The main relationships are: a donor makes many donations; an event has many registrations; a volunteer can be assigned to many events; a beneficiary receives many distributions.
