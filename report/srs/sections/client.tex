\section{Client requirements}

The basic requirements were extended by imagining a short interview with a branch coordinator and asking what makes the manual process hard. Each answer became a testable requirement linked to the module that implements it. Table~\ref{tab:client} lists them, with the screen where the user finds each one.

\toprule
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Client requirement} & \textbf{Handled by} & \textbf{Where the user finds it} \\
\midrule
CR1 & Yearly tax statement per donor, adding up the monetary donations of a year & Donation module (FR-D6) & Donations \(\rightarrow\) Yearly Statement \\
CR2 & Check the skill and availability of a volunteer before assignment & Volunteer module (FR-V4) & Volunteers \(\rightarrow\) Register to Event \\
CR3 & Certificate after the required cumulative hours & Volunteer module (FR-V5, FR-V6) & Volunteers \(\rightarrow\) Print Certificate \\
CR4 & Notify attendees when an event is updated or rescheduled & Event module, Observer (FR-E6, FR-E7) & Automatic; Events \(\rightarrow\) Send Reminder \\
CR5 & Record aid distribution per beneficiary with item, optional donation link and date & Beneficiary module (FR-B3, FR-B4) & Beneficiaries \(\rightarrow\) Distribute Aid / History \\

Further client needs covered by Section~\ref{sec:patterns} and Section~3:

\begin{itemize}
  \item find a donor quickly by name (FR-D1);
  \item print a receipt and a yearly tax statement for a donor (FR-D5, FR-D6);
  \item stop registrations when an event is full (FR-E4);
  \item tell attendees when an event changes (FR-E6);
  \item see how many hours a volunteer worked and give a certificate (FR-V5, FR-V6);
  \item see what help each beneficiary already received (FR-B4);
  \item keep a record of every message sent (FR-C3).
\end{itemize}
