\section{Non-functional requirements}

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}L{0.09\textwidth}L{0.20\textwidth}YL{0.11\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Category} & \textbf{Requirement} & \textbf{Priority} \\
\midrule
NFR-1 & Flexibility & A new receipt add-on or distribution rule shall be added by adding a new class, without editing existing classes. A new donation type or message channel requires one new class plus a small registration change. & M \\
NFR-2 & Maintainability & Code shall be split into MVC layers. Controllers contain no SQL. Data access goes through repositories; the shared database manager only opens connections and creates the schema. & M \\
NFR-3 & Reusability & Shared parts (database access, configuration, notification) shall be implemented once and reused by all modules. & M \\
NFR-4 & Usability & A staff user shall complete common tasks (add donor, record donation, register attendee) in a few steps without training. & M \\
NFR-5 & Reliability & Failed operations shall show a clear message and shall not leave half-saved data. Payments shall use a transaction. & M \\
NFR-6 & Performance & Screens shall respond in under 2 seconds for normal data sizes. & S \\
NFR-7 & Data integrity & Donors are archived, not deleted, so donation history stays valid. & M \\
NFR-8 & Portability & The application shall run on any system with Java installed. No separate database server is needed. & S \\
NFR-9 & Consistency & The graphical and the console interface shall give the same result for the same action, because they share controllers and services. & M \\
NFR-10 & Usability & Open screens shall refresh after a change, so users never see an outdated list. & M \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
