\section{Functional requirements}

Each requirement has an identifier, a priority (Must or Should), and an acceptance condition that can be checked in the running application. Requirements marked Must are part of the Phase~1 demonstration.

\subsection{Donations and donors}

\begin{center}
\footnotesize
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}L{0.08\textwidth}YL{0.08\textwidth}L{0.30\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Requirement} & \textbf{Priority} & \textbf{Acceptance} \\
\midrule
FR-D1 & Create, edit, and archive donor records with name, contact details, and type. & Must & A donor can be added, edited, archived, and found again. \\
FR-D2 & Record a donation as online, check, or in-kind. & Must & Each type saves the fields it needs and appears in the list. \\
FR-D3 & Validate donation data: positive amount, check number for checks, item and quantity for in-kind. & Must & Invalid input is rejected with a clear message. \\
FR-D4 & Filter donations by donor, type, and date range. & Must & The table shows only matching records. \\
FR-D5 & Produce a receipt with optional tax and thank-you sections. & Must & The preview contains the selected sections. \\
FR-D6 & Produce a yearly statement per donor from monetary donations. & Must & The total matches the donor's monetary donations for the year. \\
FR-D7 & Process an online card or wallet payment through a simulated provider. & Should & A payment result is recorded and linked to the donation. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
