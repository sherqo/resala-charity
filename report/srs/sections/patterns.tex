\section{Design pattern requirements (Phase 1)}
\label{sec:patterns}

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}L{0.13\textwidth}YL{0.30\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Pattern} & \textbf{Where it is required} & \textbf{Why} \\
\midrule
Singleton & Database connection, configuration, audit log. & One shared instance for the whole application. \\
Factory & Creating online, check and in-kind donations, each with its own validation; also creating the family of console screens. & Add a donation type without changing the caller. \\
Strategy & Message channels (email, SMS, social); distribution policies (equal, by need, first come first served). & Swap behavior at runtime. \\
Observer & After a donation: activity-feed entry and thank-you message. When an event is updated, opened, closed or reminded: inform the registered attendees. & Services do not need to know who reacts. \\
Decorator & Receipt with optional thank-you and tax additions. & Combine add-ons freely; two options would otherwise need four receipt subclasses. \\
MVC & Whole application: two complete interfaces (graphical and console) share the same controllers and services. & Separate screens, input handling and business rules. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

The class diagram in the Phase~1 report shows all main classes and where each pattern is applied.
